section: New features
---
- **iPhone Duo and foldables**: `Device.iphoneDuo()` builds the widget under test once for every pose of the device and puts them side by side in one golden, so a single image covers the cover display, both ways of holding it half open, and the inner display lying flat. Each pose is built from scratch, with its own `setup`, `action` and `tearDown`. `IphoneDuoPose` has three further poses — the cover display in both landscape rotations and the inner display in portrait — which `poses` picks between. The metrics of the cover display are measured on the iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.2); those of the inner display come from [`duo_kit`](https://pub.dev/packages/duo_kit), as the simulator cannot be folded from the command line. See [README § Foldables](README.md#foldables).
- **`reportsDisplayFeatures`**: Flutter fills no `MediaQuery.displayFeatures` on iOS, so an app knows where the fold is only through a plugin that publishes it, such as [`foldable`](https://pub.dev/packages/foldable). It defaults to `false`, the plain Flutter behaviour, so goldens show what an app without such a plugin does: dialogs and sheets open across the crease, and layouts cannot avoid it. Set it to `true` for an app that has one. The device frame draws the status cluster, the camera and the fold either way, and leaves the rest of the side strip to the app, where Apple's guidelines put the navigation.
- **`Device.folding`**: a foldable this package has no preset for, built out of the postures it can show. Each posture is an ordinary `Device` with a name, which the golden writes under its screen, and the folding device takes its window from the first of them, so the two cannot disagree. `Device.iphoneDuo()` is built with it.
- **`Device.displayFeatures` and `Device.postures`**: any `Device` can carry folds, hinges and cutouts, which the harness sets on the test view, and the poses to show side by side in one golden. `Device.iphoneDuo()` is built from them.
